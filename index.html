GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

What services does IMZO offer? +

IMZO designs and develops modern websites, landing pages, e-commerce experiences, apps, software and AI-powered solutions. Every project focuses on thoughtful design, usability and your business goals.

How do I start a project? +

Share your idea, requirements and estimated budget through our contact form. We'll review your enquiry and discuss the project scope, expected timeline, deliverables and next steps before work begins.

Do I have to pay before seeing my website? +

No. IMZO will show you the completed website preview before requesting payment. Before work begins, we'll clarify the project scope, deliverables and payment terms. Final handover and deployment arrangements will be explained in advance.

How long does a project take? +

Timelines depend on the project's complexity, features, content and feedback. We'll discuss a realistic estimated timeline after understanding your requirements.

How can I contact IMZO for support? +

Send us a message through our website contact form or reach out through our official Instagram account. Contact us on Instagram ↗

Privacy policy +

Information you provide

When you contact IMZO through our website, you may provide your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, service preference, estimated budget and project message. Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry.

How we use your information

We use submitted information to review your enquiry, respond to you, understand your project requirements and discuss our services.

Contact form service

Our website uses Web3Forms to process contact form submissions. Information submitted through the form may be processed or retained under the service's policies and settings. Please review the Web3Forms Privacy Policy ↗ for further information.

Data handling and sharing

Information may be processed by service providers involved in delivering our contact form and managing enquiries. We do not promise that information is never stored or processed by these services.

Your choices and privacy requests

Please do not submit passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive personal information through the contact form. For questions about information you submitted, or to request its correction or deletion, contact IMZO through our website contact form or official Instagram account. Requests will be reviewed subject to applicable requirements and necessary retention obligations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact IMZO through the website contact form or our official Instagram account ↗.

Terms of service +

1. Project enquiries

Submitting an enquiry does not automatically create a client agreement or guarantee that a project will be accepted.

2. Scope and deliverables

Before work begins, IMZO and the client should clarify the requirements, deliverables, estimated timeline, included revisions and any additional services. Changes outside the agreed scope may require a revised timeline or separate agreement.

3. Preview and payment

IMZO will show the completed website preview before requesting payment. The project scope, agreed payment terms and conditions for final handover or deployment should be communicated before work begins.

4. Client responsibilities

Clients should provide accurate project information, required content, feedback and approvals in a timely manner. Delays may affect delivery timelines.

5. Revisions and changes

Included revisions should be agreed before work begins. Major changes or additional features may require more time and a separate agreement.

6. Ownership and third-party materials

Ownership and usage rights for final deliverables should be clarified in the project agreement. Third-party fonts, software, images, libraries and other assets remain subject to their applicable licences and terms.

7. Hosting and maintenance

Hosting, domain registration, renewals, maintenance, updates and post-launch support are included only when agreed. Any additional costs and responsibilities should be communicated before the relevant work begins.

8. Cancellations and delays

If either party wishes to pause or cancel a project, both parties should communicate promptly and address completed work, outstanding obligations and applicable payments according to their agreement and applicable law.

9. Website information

We aim to keep website information accurate and up to date. Portfolio examples illustrate our work; the features and results of each project depend on its agreed scope.

10. Updates to these terms

We may update these terms when our services or business practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For questions about these terms or a proposed project, contact IMZO through the website contact form or our official Instagram account ↗.
